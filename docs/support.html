Getting Started

Agdayo works fully offline for planning a solo trip, no account needed. Create a trip from the Trips tab, then add activities, accommodations, transport, and a budget from the trip's detail screen.

Sharing a Trip

To plan with others, sign in from the Profile tab (Apple, Google, or email/password), then open your trip's Share option to get an invite code. Anyone who enters that code on the Trips tab joins the trip and sees live updates.

Live Location Sharing

On a shared trip's Map screen, tap the location icon to opt in to sharing your position with other trip members. It's foreground-only and can be turned off at any time. A floating banner always shows when it's active.

Budgets and Expenses

Budget categories represent your planned spending, not actual expenses. Add activities, accommodations, transport costs, or a standalone "Expense" entry, and optionally attach each to a category or to a specific member's individual budget to track planned-vs-actual spending.

Deleting Your Account

Go to Profile → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account, any trips you own, your membership in shared trips, and your profile from Agdayo's servers. This can't be undone.
